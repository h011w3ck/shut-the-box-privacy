Privacy Policy for Shut the Box

Last updated: October 15, 2025

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how "Shut the Box" ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your data when you use our mobile application.

2. Data Controller

Responsible for data processing:
Wolfgang Hollweck
Unterstollnkirchen 6a, 84405 Dorfen
Email: wolfgang@hollweck.org

3. Data We Collect

3.1 Game Data (Local Storage Only)

  • Game scores and completion times
  • Game statistics (times won, best scores)
  • Tutorial completion status
  • Game preferences and settings

This data is stored locally on your device using Android's SecurePreferences and is NOT transmitted to any external servers.

3.2 Google Play Games Services (Optional)

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games Services, the following data is processed by Google:

  • Your Google account information
  • Leaderboard scores (completion times)
  • Achievement data

This data is processed according to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. How We Use Your Data

4.1 Local Game Data

  • To save your game progress
  • To display your personal statistics
  • To remember your preferences
  • To show or hide the tutorial

4.2 Google Play Games Data

  • To display your scores on leaderboards
  • To track achievements
  • To enable social gaming features

5. Data Storage and Security

  • All game data is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage (SecurePreferences)
  • We do not have access to your locally stored data
  • Google Play Games data is stored on Google's servers according to their security standards
  • No data is transmitted to any servers controlled by us

6. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

6.1 Google Play Games Services

  • Purpose: Leaderboards and social features
  • Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • You can choose not to use this service; the game works fully offline

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data (locally stored on your device)
  • Delete your data (by uninstalling the app or clearing app data)
  • Opt out of Google Play Games Services (by not signing in or signing out)
  • Request information about data processing (contact us at the email above)

8. Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app is suitable for all ages and does not require any personal information to play.

9. Data Retention

  • Local game data: Retained until you uninstall the app or clear app data
  • Google Play Games data: Retained according to Google's retention policies

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at:
wolfgang@hollweck.org

12. Technical Information

The app uses:

  • Android SecurePreferences for encrypted local storage
  • Google Play Games Services v2 API
  • No analytics or tracking services
  • No advertising services
  • No data collection beyond what is described above

13. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Union:

  • Local data processing: Legitimate interest (providing game functionality)
  • Google Play Games: Your consent (you choose to sign in)

You can withdraw consent for Google Play Games at any time by signing out.
